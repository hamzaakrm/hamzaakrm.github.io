A bit about me

I'm a Software Engineering postgraduate with practical experience designing, developing, testing, and maintaining software applications - currently pursuing an MSc in Software Engineering at the University of Hertfordshire, with a strong foundation in object-oriented programming, data structures, algorithms, and software engineering principles.

I also build software independently. Aura, TruthLens and Verity are three privacy focused applications I designed and built on my own, taking each from architecture through to deployment. Verity is the newest, merging the other two into a single assistant that remembers you and verifies what it tells you.

Most recently I worked remotely as a Generalist Expert at Mercor (Aug – Sep 2026), evaluating AI model output for factual grounding, relevance, and reasoning quality - close-reading work that sits on the other side of the pipeline from building.

I'm currently open to software development and engineering roles where I can combine practical, hands-on coding experience with a genuine interest in building things that matter.

03 · Projects

Verity

↗
Latest

A personal AI assistant that remembers you and checks what it tells you. I built it by merging Aura and TruthLens into one application, then took it further. It routes each message to either local chat or a research pipeline, answers with citations and a confidence label counted from genuinely independent sources, and keeps follow up questions on the sources it already fetched. It also verifies images, holds a voice conversation, and runs on your own machine with an optional cloud model behind a single toggle.
